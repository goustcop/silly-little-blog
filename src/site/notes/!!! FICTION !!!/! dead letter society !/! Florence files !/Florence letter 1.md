<center>Dear sophia,</center>

As im sure your already aware, my name is Florence. Florence Cora Poeseph. Im the wife of the duke George Poeseph, well wife is a bit of a misleading term. You see, i do swap between genders from time to time. Sometimes i wear corset, sometimes i wear undervests. Most mortals dont understand that concept, so he knows me as his wife, but Im fine being referred to any which way. 

I wanted to recount to you a bit of my day, as it is my first letter, and i hope you may assit me in finding some clarity. I tend to spend my days in the castle library. Its a long hall filled top to bottom with books. Ladders line the wall with seats and tables littering the center. I decided to wear a beautiful baby blue day dress, although it went slightly above my ankles, but its not a crime if no one sees. Dont tell the church, they may think im a witch, or worse a vampire. I kid, yet i digress. I had walked up to the bookshelf to look for a particular poets work, unsure of where i left it last. I do not tend to the orgainzation of my books, a bad habit i suppose. nonetheless I searched tirelessly, until i came upon a new book i had never seen before. Its was a red leather bound book with nothing on its cover, but the binding had an odd mark, remincient of the dead letter societies symbol. I grabbed it to read it, but just as i was about to, Esmerelda entered the library. She wore a cotton dress, apron on top with frills on the sholders. My husband tell her to choose a less 'gaudy' appron, but i personally enjoy the self expression. In her hands was a tray with earl gray tea, still steaming. I put the book back instantly, i do not know why, she would simply assuume it was a book about death, hense the skull embelisment. When she came up to me, we had tea together, we laughed, and i showed her some poems i had written previously. It was a fun day.

I simply ask, do you know of any book similar to this? And how may it have found its way into my library. The situation with the book left me slightly shaken, so i would love some clerification. 

I look fondly towards your response.
<center>
Your newest friend, 
Florence cora</center>
