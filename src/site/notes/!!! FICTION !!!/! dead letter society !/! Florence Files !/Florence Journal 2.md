I had a dreadful nightmare today. It was about George again, the exact same dream. The most terrifying part was alwas seeing him on the stake, but this time i could feel the flames from across the crowd. They gathered to kill him that day, i will never know why.

I miss george, an awful lot. He was the only other person i could trust. No human nor vampire will ever replace him. And now i go back to the society, begging for friendship. I feel pathetic.

Ive just felt lost, ive felt lost for a while. No amount of storys and literature i read will ever hold a match to that of my loves writing. George always knew what he was doing and where he was going. I envyed that, and now that hes gone...

im sorry i just cant. I know its been years. Im trying.

Ive been trying for a very long time.

Theres nothing i can do anymore. I have to try to wake up in the mornings, try to dress myself in layer upon layer for the apperences when i dont see anyone anymore. Im lonely.

I know the only way out is to reach out first. And thats what im doing with the society. I paid my dues. I just want to find other vampires. i plan on cutting off the society as soon as possible, i just need to find other communitys. I cant trust mortals. You can never trust mortals. Even Esmeralda, who i will admit is different, just an innocent old woman. I wish i could be the same, i apire to be more like her every day.

Besides, Gorge would want me to forgive and forget, right?
