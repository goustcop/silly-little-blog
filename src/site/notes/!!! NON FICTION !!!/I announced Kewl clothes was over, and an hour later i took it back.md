So i posted a story a bit ago announcing the death of kewl clothes. 

That didn't last long.

There was a big kick off party planned, with a photo shoot for social media content and event decorations and everything. That was until the day before the photo shoot, everyone dropped from the project. it was just me and my ideas again. 

I went to my therapist about this, and she told me to flat out give up. Worst therapist ever, but i believed her and i posted a story so no more people would donate to the fundraiser since kewl clothes was over. Something still didn't feel right, So i called my aunt who owns an amazing skincare business. She gave me some advice that really applied, and i think anyone who wants to start a project should learn from this.

She told me that i hadn't done anything yet. I had only 3 posts on Instagram and 2 of them were announcing the event and the business. Only 1 of them was actual content. i was asking people to believe in something that didn't exist yet. It made me notice that i felt i needed those other people to make it in the first place, cause i didn't have the confidence to let the project stand on its own two legs. That was my problem. I didn't believe in my project and was relying on other people to make it feel real.

So im going back to the original plan, just show up at raves or parties with a booth dedicated to helping you fix your clothes where they tend to break. And that what we are gonna do. The socials will be full of DIY tricks, information on clothes and sustainability, and artist shout outs. I'm not abandoning the concept of the community being a part if the process, I'm just giving it a new form.

So yes, kewl clothes is still happening, just a little smaller in the beginning. I was focused too much on getting a bunch of people interested, instead of getting a smaller but more loyal group of followers to help propel me in the future. I was focusing on the short term. 

I cant wait for people to believe in kewl clothes when i didn't believe in it myself. Thats what im changing, thats whats gonna help me make progress towards my goals. So if your starting a project, start small. Build what you can with what you have, you don't need to wait for permission.
