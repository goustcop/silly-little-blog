Dear florence, </center>

Welcome to the soceity my dear. 

My name is Sophie, i was assigned by the society to foster connections in our community as well as to simply check in. We do care quite a bit about each of our memebers well being. It isnt easy to find people like us, so we have to really take care of each other. 

Thank you for sharing your day. Im sure tea must have been lovely, The book however is odd. Last i remeber a printed book from the society was made around 100 years ago, ill have to recount the files. I would be able to help better if you send the book with your next letter. 

As it is only fair, ill share about my day to day. I work under the society as what i call a proffesional organizer. I organize and summarize and clean up offical docuements.  I cannot share any details as most of it is classified, but what i can tell you is about a coworker I had a run in with a few days ago. Her name was opal and we had lunch together, She offered me some of her cake which she had brought from home. it was a delicious red velvet with cream cheese frosting. We spoke a bit and she gave me a new perspective on a work issue. It was nice. I havent seen her since then, since we havent been assigned the same projects for a while. 

The most intresting part of my day was when i picked up a pair of lovely handmade stone dice. I know, that being the most intresting part of my day? Must have been a boring day, and to be honest it wasPerhaps when we eventually see each other in person, we can play a game with them. I know quite a few story building games and since we are both fans of the literary arts, i figured it would be fun. 

Get back to me on the book, as that intrests me, and let me know when we can plan our first outing. 
<center>
From,
Sophie</center>
