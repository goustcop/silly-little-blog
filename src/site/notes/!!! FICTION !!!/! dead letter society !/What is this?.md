# DEAD LETTER SOCIETY is a *solo letting game* about vampires

And I'm currently playing it! The way it works if you create a character according to the manual, then use tarot cards or number generators to come up with plot twists, character arcs, and more. You play two characters that are constantly effecting each other and you just work with the cards you get forcing you to get creative. That's exactly the reason i love these types of games. I have a few solo lettering games and I've played a woefully small amount of them. I'm trying to change that, and what better motivation then to force other people to get invested in your little characters too?

I wont say much about the story, but it takes place in Victorian England. Its a little Lovecraft, little vampirism, has some secret societies, and a lot of doomed Yuri.

<center>So join me, join the dead letter society</center>
